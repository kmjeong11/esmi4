Status & Cause Definitions

Status
Unavoidable — Alarms that cannot be completely eliminated due to operator errors, sensor damage, or equipment failures.
Operational — A normal operation alarm that is not caused by a fault. (Ex: EMO Switch On alarm)
On-going — The alarm has been identified, and corrective actions and rollout are in progress.
Monitoring — Under 10-day monitoring after corrective actions and rollout.
Completed — No alarm recurrence during the 10-day monitoring period.
Cause (Root-Cause Category)
Equipment — Alarms caused by a hardware fault or malfunction of the equipment itself: sensors, cylinders, motors, drives, actuators, or other mechanical parts failing, drifting out of spec, or losing alignment.
Operator — Alarms triggered by operator actions or manual intervention during production, such as running in manual mode, opening equipment doors, or an object being detected while a worker is inside or working on the line.
Material — Alarms caused by the condition of materials or consumables — jigs, pallets, cells, trays, or foils — including defects, damage, misalignment, poor seating/loading, or foreign objects.
IT — Alarms related to IT systems, networking, communication, or software: MES/CIM interface errors, RFID / PLC / barcode communication faults, and control-sequence or parameter issues.
Process Equipment — Alarms that originate from the upstream or downstream process equipment interfacing with the line (e.g., stockers, aging/formation units, adjacent process tools) rather than from the conveyance equipment itself.
Utility — Alarms caused by instability or interruption of utility supply, such as compressed air, electrical power, vacuum, or cooling water.
Environment — Alarms caused by environmental or external factors, such as temperature/humidity conditions, static, vibration, or external electrical interference.
Unknown — The root cause has not been clearly identified yet. These alarms are kept under monitoring so the trend can be observed over time and the true cause confirmed before a category is assigned.
